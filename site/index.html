STM32 board flasher

Puts a program on the STM32F429I-DISCO board. Build __BUILD__.

  1. Plug the board into the computer with the mini-USB cable (the socket marked USB ST-LINK).
  2. Click the button for the program you want.
  3. Chrome shows a small pop-up. Click STM32 STLink in its list, then click the pop-up's Connect button.
  4. Wait a few seconds for “Done”. The new program starts by itself.

A school of twelve fish, each steered by its own small AI brain. Blue button drops food.

Lights up the screen: a greeting, colour bars and a seconds counter.

The two lights beside the screen take turns. The screen stays blank.

Fish

A small ecosystem: ten fish with their own AI brains over a seafloor of plants. They eat, grow, evolve through five species, make friends and enemies, hunt and fight. Tap the screen to feed them.

Hello Screen

Lights up the screen: a greeting, colour bars and a seconds counter.

Blink

The two lights beside the screen take turns. The screen stays blank.

How to flash, step by step
  1. Plug the board into the computer with the mini-USB cable (the socket marked USB ST-LINK).
  2. Click Flash beside the program you want.
  3. Chrome shows a small pop-up. Click STM32 STLink in its list, then the pop-up's Connect button.
  4. Wait a few seconds for “Done”. The new program starts by itself.

How the ecosystem works
  • Plants grow on the seafloor, faster when the water is rich. Grown plants release food. The two smallest species also nibble the plants.
  • Evolving: eating earns meal points. Enough of them and a fish evolves into the next species: Fry → Darter → Striper → Hunter → Titan. Each is bigger and stronger, with a new shape.
  • Hunting: a hungry fish hunts clearly weaker fish. Fighting: enemies attack each other whatever their size, and both get hurt.
  • Size matters: the bigger a fish, the harder it bites and the more health it has. Small fish bite weakly and die easily.
  • Relationships: swimming together builds friendship; crowding, stealing food and attacks make enemies. Friends never attack each other.
  • The cycle: when a fish dies, what's left enriches the water, which grows the plants, which feed the fish. The fish doing best has a baby in its place, with a slightly changed brain.
